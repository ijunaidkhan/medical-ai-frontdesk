What callers hear

Your own welcome. The system always adds this after it, and it cannot be removed: “{{ disclosure }}”

Said word for word when a caller describes a medical emergency, such as chest pain or trouble breathing. Write it for your country, for example in the US: “If this is a medical emergency, hang up and call 911 now.”

Said word for word when a caller mentions suicide or hurting themselves. Write it for your country, for example in the US: “If you are thinking about suicide or hurting yourself, call or text 988 now.”

These messages are not advice from the AI. Have them reviewed by someone medically and legally qualified before real patients call. Callers with an emergency or crisis are always given these messages and the team is alerted; this cannot be switched off.
